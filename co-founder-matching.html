Co-Founder Matching

Your next partner is already in this community.

Building a career is difficult if you go it alone. Whether you're a seasoned entrepreneur with multiple exits, or you just have the drive to build something, the right partner can change everything.

How it works

A real human reviews every match.

NYC Fintech Women's Co-Founder Matching program connects ambitious women across our 10,000+ member network who are ready to create together. Fill out the form and our in-house matchmaker will reach out with a curated recommendation based on your goals, skills, and vision.
